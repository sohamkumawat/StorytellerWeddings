Corporate Agreement

Corporate Booking Terms

Enterprise billing protocols, crew allocation guidelines, and digital deliverable timelines.

Key Protocol: Crew calendar dates and multi-track production crews are locked upon SOW signing and retainer confirmation.

1. Production Retainer & Crew Scheduling

Corporate production schedules and camera crews are reserved upon execution of the Statement of Work (SOW) and receipt of a 50% mobilization retainer (or approved Net-30 enterprise PO for registered corporate partners).

2. Delivery & Turnaround SLA

  • Live / Same-Day PR Selects: 20–50 color-graded selects delivered within 4–12 hours for immediate press and social releases.
  • Complete Archival Library: Fully curated, high-resolution commercial archive delivered within 3–5 business days.

3. Cancellation & Postponement

In the event of summit rescheduling or venue change, notice must be provided in writing at least 7 calendar days prior to event commencement to facilitate crew re-allocation without forfeiture of retainer fees.
