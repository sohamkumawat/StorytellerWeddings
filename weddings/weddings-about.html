About The Studio

Honest Storytelling, Refined Vision

Based in Pune, India and traveling nationwide. We exist to preserve authentic intimacy, familial legacy, and unscripted emotion through an editorial lens.

Philosophy

Not just photographs, but heirlooms of how it truly felt.

At Storyteller Weddings, we believe wedding photography should feel as authentic twenty years from now as it did on the day it occurred. We resist fleeting color fads and aggressive staging in favor of natural light, rich contrast, and genuine emotional resonance.

By observing rather than directing, we create space for you to remain fully present in your celebration. Every collection we deliver is curated with intention—designed to evoke the laughter, tears, and unspoken connections that define your story.

Client FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Where are you based and do you travel for weddings?

We are headquartered in Pune, Maharashtra, but document weddings across India (Udaipur, Jaipur, Goa, Kerala, Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru) and internationally. Travel and accommodation are seamlessly included in our destination commissions.

How would you describe your photographic style?

Our aesthetic is rooted in timeless documentary photojournalism merged with an understated editorial eye. We focus on genuine human connection, atmosphere, candid moments, and artful portraits without stiff poses.

What is your turnaround time for the wedding gallery?

A curated preview collection of 40–60 edited images is delivered within 7 business days following your celebration. The complete high-resolution collection and heirloom album design are finalized within 6–8 weeks.

How do we reserve our wedding date?

Dates are secured upon execution of our standard commission agreement and a retainer payment. Because we limit our calendar to preserve quality, dates are reserved on a first-confirmed basis.
