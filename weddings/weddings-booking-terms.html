Commission Agreement

Booking Terms

Transparent guidelines regarding date reservation, payment schedules, travel logistics, and archival delivery.

Key Summary: Dates are confirmed on a first-come, first-served basis upon contract signing and retainer payment.

1. Retainer & Date Reservation

To secure your wedding date in our calendar, a non-refundable retainer fee of 50% of the total commission agreement is required alongside the signed photography agreement. Until both requirements are fulfilled, dates remain open to other inquiries.

2. Payment Schedule

  • 50% Retainer: Due upon booking confirmation to secure the date.
  • 40% Milestone: Due 14 calendar days prior to the first event date.
  • 10% Final Balance: Due upon delivery of the final high-resolution gallery.

3. Travel & Logistics Policy

For destination celebrations outside Pune, all flight, transit, and accommodation arrangements are itemized transparently in your bespoke proposal. Our principal team arrives at destination locations a minimum of 24 hours prior to festivities.

4. Gallery Curation & Archival Delivery

- Editorial Preview: 40–60 curated high-resolution images within 7 business days.
- Complete Archival Gallery: Delivered via encrypted online vault within 6–8 weeks.
- Fine-Art Heirlooms: Hand-bound albums are designed in collaboration with you after gallery delivery.
