Corporate Privacy & NDA Policy

Strict protocols safeguarding enterprise intellectual property, proprietary presentations, and executive confidentiality.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Commercial Desk: Storyteller Corporate & Events, Pune, India.

1. Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDA) & Embargo Protocols

We routinely operate under unilateral or bilateral NDAs for pre-launch keynotes, unreleased product launches, and closed-door leadership summits. Images subject to press embargoes are held in air-gapped storage until the public announcement release time.

2. Data Collection & Corporate Contacts

Information submitted during the RFP process is used exclusively for proposal estimation, project logistics, invoicing, and contract execution. Client contacts and vendor networks are strictly confidential.

3. Secure Cloud Delivery & Access Controls

Commercial galleries are hosted on enterprise-grade encrypted servers with granular password authentication, restricted download permissions, and timed expiration links.

4. Contact Our Commercial Legal Desk

For custom enterprise vendor onboarding, vendor security questionnaires, or MSA execution, please reach out to storytellers.wedding@gmail.com.
