Commission Agreement

Booking Terms

Transparent guidelines regarding date reservation, payment schedules, travel logistics, and archival delivery.

Key Summary: Dates are confirmed on a first-come, first-served basis upon contract signing and retainer payment.

1. Retainer & Date Reservation

To secure your wedding or event date in our calendar, a non-refundable retainer fee of 50% of the total commission agreement is required alongside the signed photography agreement. Until both requirements are fulfilled, dates remain open to other inquiries.

2. Payment Schedule

  • 50% Retainer: Due upon booking confirmation to secure the date.
  • 40% Milestone: Due 14 calendar days prior to the first event date.
  • 10% Final Balance: Due upon delivery of the final high-resolution gallery.

3. Rescheduling & Cancellations

In the event of date postponement due to unforeseen circumstances, the retainer may be transferred to a new date within 12 months, subject to studio availability. If the new date is unavailable, standard cancellation terms apply as outlined in your bespoke contract.

4. Travel, Accommodation & Logistics

For destination weddings and assignments outside Pune, travel expenses (flights/trains and local airport transfers) and suitable hotel accommodations for the photography crew are covered by the client as specified in the agreed quote.

5. Delivery Timeline & Archival Guarantee

We guarantee delivery of your sneak-peek highlight gallery (30–50 images) within 5 business days. The complete master gallery is delivered within 4 to 6 weeks. All delivered photographs are securely backed up on cloud archives for 24 months.

6. Questions Regarding Commission Terms

For clarification on contract clauses or custom corporate agreements, please contact our studio directly at storytellers.wedding@gmail.com.
