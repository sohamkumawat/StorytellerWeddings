Privacy Policy

Your personal moments, family data, and imagery are treated with the highest standard of confidentiality and care.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Studio Base: Storyteller Weddings, Pune, Maharashtra, India.

1. Information We Collect

When you inquire with Storyteller Weddings via our website form, direct email (storytellers.wedding@gmail.com), or WhatsApp (+91 99237 68007 / +91 99237 68003), we collect the following information:

  • Full Name and Contact Number.
  • Event dates, venues, cities, and itinerary notes.
  • Billing and transaction details required for invoicing and contractual agreements.

2. How Your Information Is Used

We use your personal information solely for the purposes of:

  • Responding to your commission inquiry and preparing bespoke proposals.
  • Executing photography services, travel logistics, and contract administration.
  • Delivering private online galleries, proofing materials, and physical albums.

We will never sell, rent, or distribute your personal contact information or guest lists to any third-party marketing entities.

3. Image Archival & Privacy Rights (NDA / Private Weddings)

We respect that certain weddings, executive gatherings, and private portraits require complete discretion. If you require a non-disclosure agreement (NDA) or strict privacy concerning the publication of your images online or on social feeds, please indicate this during your initial consultation. Private commissions with full non-publication guarantees are available upon request.

4. Data Storage & Gallery Security

High-resolution client galleries are hosted on password-protected, encrypted cloud servers. Access links are provided directly to the commissioning client, who may choose to share access with family or guests at their discretion.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to update your stored contact information, please reach out to our studio at storytellers.wedding@gmail.com.
